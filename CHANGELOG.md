# Changes

## 1.6.13 — initial public release

- Three resizable, aligned readouts: Master TC, In-Out Duration, Total Duration.
- Local OCR of user-selected viewer regions during playback.
- Approximate playback timecode with frame digits replaced by dashes.
- Held duration values shown in orange.
- Settings opened by double-click or the context menu.
- Automatic capture pacing or targets of 2, 4 or 8 reads per second.
- Capture-area preview, saved areas and saved window placement.
- Settings stays above other windows.
- Closing the main timecode window quits the app.

The binary is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. macOS may require Screen
Recording authorization again after an update. Recognition latency,
playback detection and full-screen behavior depend on the local setup.
